Questions

What people ask before starting

Do I really pay nothing until I see it?

Yes. We build a demo of your idea first and send you the link. You look at it on your own phone, show it to family, ask for changes. If it is not what you imagined, you tell us and you do not pay. Price is agreed only after you are happy with the direction.

How long does a website take?

A starter site usually takes a few days once we have your content and photos. A full business system with ordering or tracking takes longer, and we build it in stages so you see progress instead of silence.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, your phone number, your prices, and any photos you have. If you do not have professional photos, we work with what you have and make it look good. A voice note describing what you want is enough to begin.

Can I change prices myself later?

Yes. Business system builds include a private panel where you change prices, stock, services and photos yourself, from your phone. You do not need to call anyone for small changes.

Do you also do Google Maps and physical work?

Yes. We set up your Google Business Profile so customers find you by name and area, and we fabricate and install aluminium and glass windows, doors and shop fronts after measuring on site.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money to the same number you call, in stages that we agree before work starts. You always know what you are paying for before you pay it.
